ABOUT THE ARTIST

RAGINI CHATTERJEE

Ragini Chatterjee is a Bangalore/Paris-based data scientist who finds balance through art. She has had the privilege of painting for others, transforming their stories into visual expressions. Through her work, she has learned to channel emotions and experiences onto canvas—creating pieces that speak what words cannot.
