TRAPPED

ACRYLIC ON CANVAS . ROLLED . 77 INCHES x 49.5 INCHES

-INSPIRATION-


The painting depicts a girl who is stuck inside a bubble that distorts the world, making it appear upside down. The girl has her hand on the bubble wanting to break it but is unable to do so. The painting was inspired by how humans are often trapped by their own psyche.
